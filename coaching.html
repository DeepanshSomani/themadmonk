Private Coaching

For those who are

This is not a course. Not a membership. Not a group call. A private, structured, deeply personal practice — built around your life, your mind, and where you actually are right now.

3

Format
Video call
Type

Each Session

The Structure

10'

A Talk

Drawing from the world's deepest traditions. Practical, not academic. Something you carry into your week.

20'

Guided Meditation

Live, responsive, not a recording. Adapted to what is present for you that day.

10'

Debrief

What came up, what shifted, what to watch for before the next session.

Daily

Independent Practice

20 minutes on non-session days. Accountability built in. A curated selection of books shared along the way.

The 30-Minute Conversation

Before anything begins, we talk. Thirty minutes. You tell me where you are, what you have tried, and what you are looking for. I tell you whether I think I can help. If the answer is yes, we begin. No sales pitch.

Who this is for

  • Successful people who have run out of external problems to solve
  • People who have tried meditation before and found it did not stick
  • People who are skeptical of spirituality but suspect there is something in it
  • People who want results, not rituals

Who this is not for

  • People looking for candles and chanting
  • People who want to be told what they want to hear
  • People who will not show up consistently
  • People who are not ready to look inward honestly
