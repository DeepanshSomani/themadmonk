The Philosophy

Osho called it Zorba the Buddha. The West gave us people who live fully but without awareness. The East gave us people who are aware but half-dead. The synthesis is the only path worth walking.

This is not about sitting still and escaping your life. It is about bringing a quality of witnessing to everything you already do — your ambitions, your relationships, your desires, your chaos.
